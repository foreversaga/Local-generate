# Reference Pose Language

Use this example to describe a rear, close bed-level four-point support pose without letting the generator simplify it into upright kneeling or sitting on the heels.

## Action and body geometry

The adult subject actively bears weight on both palms and both knees. Both knees are at least shoulder-width apart. The hips are higher than the knees and fully lifted away from the heels and calves; the buttocks do not touch the heels or calves. Both palms press into the bed one forearm-length ahead of the shoulder line, fingers spread, wrists in front of the shoulders, and arms extended. The torso leans distinctly forward from the hips and the back is close to horizontal. The palms never rest beside the hips, and the subject is not sitting on the heels or kneeling upright. Only the face is relaxed; the body maintains active four-point support.

## Camera and crop

The camera is directly behind the subject at 180 degrees, about 0.8 metres away, at pelvis height and close to the bed surface. Use the perspective of a handheld phone main camera around 26mm equivalent: the hips are the closest and visibly largest foreground plane, while the shoulders and head recede. Use a vertical 2:3 frame from the head to the lower thighs. Knees may touch the bottom edge, but the lower calves, ankles, and feet stay outside the frame. The bed surface and headboard remain readable.

## Reference-preserving variation

Face, hairstyle, and clothing may change. The support points, joint geometry, separation between hips and heels, rear camera position, perspective, visible rear-facing surfaces, and crop do not change.
